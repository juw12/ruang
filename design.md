# Ruang design notes

Identity: an architect's drafting table, not a SaaS dashboard.

- Paper `#f4f1ea` background, ink `#1e2328` text, one accent - terracotta `#a84b25` (roof tile / red pencil). No purple, no gradients, no glass cards.
- Type: serif headings (Georgia stack, editorial), monospace for every measurement, price and label (feels like a drawing dimension).
- Title block: the header is laid out like the title block in the corner of a drawing - project name, scale, region, date.
- Lines: 1 px ink rules and hairline grids instead of shadows. Corners are square or 2 px.
- Steps read like sheets of a drawing set: 1 Photo, 2 Look, 3 Arrange, 4 Renovate, 5 Cost, 6 Tips.
- One primary button per step (accent fill). Everything else is ink outline.
- Numbers always show unit and source; estimates show a range, never a single figure.
- Mobile 390 px: panel docks under the 3D view, no horizontal scroll.
